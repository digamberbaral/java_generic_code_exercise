Hybrid Methodology — 5 Systems Fused
Each system targets a specific athletic quality

Ronnie Coleman Volume

"Lightweight baby!" Heavy compounds for HIGH REPS (8–12+). Not just singles. 5×10 squats at 130kg with 90s rest builds more stamina than any cardio. Volume = work capacity = stamina under load.

Jujimufu Flexibility

Strength + splits = real athleticism. Loaded pancakes, PNF-loaded front splits, Jefferson curls. Stretch AFTER heavy training when muscles are hot. Load makes flexibility permanent.

Ido Portal Movement

"Move or die." Daily hanging (7 min), squat sits (5 min), locomotion flows. Movement quality as the foundation of all athletic expression. Ground-based preparation.

Frank Medrano Calisthenics

Bodyweight mastery at any size. Weighted muscle-ups, front levers, human flags — integrated INTO heavy training. Strength expressed through bodyweight control.

DDP Yoga Recovery

"Ain't your mama's yoga." Dynamic tension stretching — flex all muscles WHILE in stretch positions. Active recovery that builds real flexibility. Rehabilitated wheelchair-bound athletes.

Ben Patrick KOT

Bulletproof joints for 175kg+ loads. ATG Split Squats, Tibialis work, Nordic Curls. Loaded heavier to match your strength. Joint prep is non-negotiable at this weight class.

The Hybrid Principle: Every session blends 2+ systems. Coleman squats finish with Jujimufu loaded stretches. Ido hangs open every session. DDP flows close every session. You never do "just strength" or "just mobility."

Recovery Science — Will You Actually Recover?
The honest answer: YES — but only if you follow these non-negotiable protocols
The Reality Check: You're training 6 days/week with 175kg+ compounds, calisthenics skills, loaded stretching, AND conditioning. Without deliberate recovery management, you WILL burn out by Week 3. The program is designed with built-in recovery mechanisms — but you must execute them.
Why This Program WORKS Despite Intensity
  • Never 2 heavy days back-to-back Mon upper / Tue lower
  • Wednesday = Movement + Conditioning Active recovery for Mon/Tue
  • Thursday = Calisthenics + Hypertrophy Low spinal load
  • Friday = Heavy lower again 72+ hrs after Tuesday
  • Sunday = Full yoga recovery Parasympathetic reset
  • Deload every 4th week Mandatory supercompensation
  • 90s rest (not 30s) between heavy sets ATP partial recovery
  • DDP Yoga = active recovery built-in Not passive rest
Recovery Red Flags — STOP If You See These
  • Resting HR elevated 5+ bpm above normal Overreaching
  • Lifts declining 2 weeks in a row Take extra rest day
  • Joint pain (NOT muscle soreness) Skip that movement
  • Sleep quality dropping Reduce volume 30%
  • Mood/motivation crashed CNS fatigue — deload NOW
  • Can't complete warm-up sets properly Go home. Rest.
  • DOMS lasting 4+ days Too much volume
  • Appetite vanished Cortisol spike — back off
THE 5 RECOVERY PILLARS (ALL MANDATORY)
Pillar 1: Sleep (The #1 Recovery Tool)
  • Minimum sleep 7.5–9 hours
  • Sleep schedule consistency ±30 min daily
  • Room temperature 18–20°C
  • No screens 60 min before bed Blue light = cortisol
  • Magnesium glycinate before bed 400mg
  • Growth Hormone release during deep sleep This is when muscle BUILDS
Pillar 2: Nutrition Timing
  • Post-workout protein 40–50g within 30 min
  • Post-workout carbs 60–80g fast carbs
  • Pre-sleep casein/cottage cheese 30g slow protein
  • Total daily protein 1.6g/lb (split 4–5 meals)
  • Hydration 3.5–4L water daily min
  • Collagen 30 min pre-training 15g + Vit C (tendon repair)
Pillar 3: Active Recovery
  • Sunday DDP Yoga 40 min (mandatory)
  • Daily walking 8000+ steps
  • Foam rolling post-session 5 min major groups
  • Cold shower (last 60s) Inflammation control
  • Contrast shower (heavy days) 30s cold / 60s hot × 3
Pillar 4: Stress Management
  • 4-7-8 Breathing daily 5 min (cortisol down)
  • Training stress + life stress = TOTAL Account for both
  • High work stress week? Drop 1 conditioning set
  • Caffeine cutoff 2 PM latest
  • Sunlight first 30 min of day Circadian rhythm
Pillar 5: Periodization
  • Week 4: MANDATORY deload Supercompensation
  • Week 8: MANDATORY deload Peak adaptation
  • Never max effort 2 days in a row CNS needs 48–72hrs
  • Listen to RPE — if 9+ too often Back off 10%
  • Auto-regulate: bad day = reduce load Not every day is PR day
The Science of Why This Works:

Muscle Protein Synthesis (MPS) peaks 24–48 hours post-training and returns to baseline by 72 hours. Your schedule ensures:
• Upper body (Mon) → 72+ hours before next upper (Thu) ✅
• Heavy lower (Tue) → 72+ hours before next heavy lower (Fri) ✅
• Wed/Sun are LOW spinal-load days → spine recovers from squat/deadlift ✅
• Loaded stretching (Jujimufu) INCREASES recovery by promoting blood flow to trained muscles ✅
• DDP Yoga (Sun) activates parasympathetic system → accelerates systemic recovery ✅

Key insight: The conditioning and mobility work on Wed/Sun are NOT additional STRESS — they are RECOVERY ACCELERATORS. Low-intensity movement pushes blood into damaged tissue faster than sitting on a couch. This is why active athletes recover faster than sedentary people.
Supplementation for Recovery at This Intensity:
• Creatine: 5g daily (ATP regeneration between sets)
• Collagen: 15g + 50mg Vitamin C pre-training (tendon/fascia repair)
• Magnesium Glycinate: 400mg before bed (muscle relaxation + sleep quality)
• Omega-3: 3g EPA/DHA daily (systemic inflammation control)
• Zinc: 30mg daily (testosterone + immune support)
• Vitamin D3: 4000 IU daily (muscle function + recovery hormones)
• Optional: Tart Cherry Juice 250ml post-training (reduces DOMS by 25% per research)
THE AUTO-REGULATION RULE: If you feel beaten up by Week 2–3, DO NOT power through. Use this adjustment ladder:

1. First: Reduce conditioning rounds by 50% (Wed + Sat)
2. Second: Drop volume sets (remove the 2×15 squat endurance set)
3. Third: Make Thursday a full DDP Yoga day instead of training
4. Fourth: If still fatigued → take an unplanned deload (3–4 days at 50%)

The program assumes you're eating 3500+ calories, sleeping 8+ hours, and managing life stress. If ANY of those are compromised, reduce training load FIRST.

Progression & Deload Rules
When to push, when to back off
Progression Rules
  • Hit all reps all sets? +2.5–5kg next week
  • Volume set felt easy? +5kg immediately
  • Calisthenics hold exceeded? Next progression level
  • Deeper than last week? +2.5kg stretch load
  • Sprint improved? Add distance (40m→50m)
  • RPE below 7? Increase load now
  • Joint pain? STOP. Rest. Reassess.
Deload Protocol (Wk 4, 8)
  • Strength volume –50%
  • Strength load –30–40%
  • KOT Protocol NEVER deloads
  • Flexibility time INCREASE 50%
  • DDP Yoga Add 2nd session
  • Conditioning 3 rounds max
  • Calisthenics Skill only, no load
Golden Rule: Deload weeks = flexibility acceleration. Body recovers from load stress → push DEEPER into stretches, LONGER holds, FURTHER movement patterns. Strength deloads. Flexibility never does.
